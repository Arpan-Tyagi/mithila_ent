## 3. Competitive Gap Analysis

### Major Competitors in India:
1. **Fabriclore (D2C / Semi-Wholesale):** Broad marketplace model with high MOQ hurdles for custom print runs. Weakness: lacks single-bolt continuous cut guarantee; higher blended synthetic footprint.
2. **HP Singh / Nehru Place Retailers:** Traditional retail store, high offline traffic, fragmented digital presence, opaque online stock allocation.
3. **Gandhi Nagar / Shanti Mohalla Traditional Traders:** Lowest price, but zero digital ordering, cash-heavy, lack standardized shrink/color-fastness test data.

### Mithila Enterprises Differentiators (USP to emphasize across all channels):
1. **Fractional Precision:** Meterage sold in 0.5m increments — perfect for sampling, fashion students, bespoke tailors, and luxury capsules.
2. **Single-Bolt Continuity:** Guaranteed uninterrupted continuous cut (no fragmented remnants stitched together).
3. **Madhubani Heritage Infusion:** Authentic motifs and vegetable dye traditions rooted in Bihar's ancient artisan knowledge.
4. **Radical Traceability:** Direct weaver cooperative sourcing from Bhagalpur, Maheshwar, and Pochampally.
5. **Technical Transparency:** Transparent yarn count (Lea), exact GSM, weave construction, and wash-care shrinkage parameters on every fabric listing.
