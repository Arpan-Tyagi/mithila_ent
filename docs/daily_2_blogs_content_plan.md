## 3. Detailed Structural Blueprint for Daily Articles (E-E-A-T & Google Quality Gate Compliance)

To pass Google's **Helpful Content System** and **E-E-A-T Quality Gates** (min 1,500 words, 100% original, verified technical precision):

### Anatomy of Every Morning Article ("The Fabric Masterclass"):
1. **Direct Answer / Definition Lead (0–80 words):** A concise, bolded summary directly answering the search query for Google AI Overviews and snippet extraction.
2. **Technical Parameter Quick-Card:** Table stating exact Lea count / GSM / fiber purity / weave structure / shrinkage % / recommended needle gauge.
3. **Deep Scientific / Material Breakdown (400–600 words):** The physics and chemistry of the fiber.
4. **Practical Workshop / Cutting Guide (400 words):** Pattern-making advice, cutting techniques, interfacing compatibility, and tailoring warnings.
5. **Wash, Iron & Care Protocol (250 words):** Pre-wash rules and thermal memory.
6. **Commercial Sourcing & Swatch CTA:** Direct link to order fractional cuts or request wholesale bolts.

### Anatomy of Every Evening Article ("The Artisan Archive"):
1. **Cultural Narrative Hook (0–100 words):** Sensory description of the loom floor or dye workshop.
2. **Historical & Anthropological Lineage (400 words):** Origin myths, regional geography, and intergenerational transmission.
3. **The Living Process (500 words):** Step-by-step documentation of the artisan technique (fermentation, warping, block carving).
4. **Artisan Perspective & Economic Reality (300 words):** Wage transparency, cluster challenges, and generational preservation.
5. **Modern Runway / Interior Application (250 words):** How contemporary designers incorporate this motif.

## 4. Guardrails Against Keyword Cannibalization & Quality Penalties

When publishing 60 articles in 30 days, follow these 4 non-negotiable rules:
1. **One Primary Keyword Per Article:** No two articles ever share the exact same primary search query.
2. **Distinct URL Slugs & Canonical Anchors:** Every article must possess a unique, keyword-optimized slug and canonical URL.
3. **Intent Separation:**
   - If an article is about *Linen 40 Lea*, it does NOT attempt to rank for *Summer Linens 80 Lea*.
   - If an article is about *Natural Indigo Chemistry*, it does NOT compete with *Madhubani Print Motifs*.
4. **Internal Linking Spine:** Every blog post links:
   - Upward to the relevant parent category page (`/category/linen`, `/category/cotton`, etc.)
   - Downward to 2 sibling cluster articles.
   - Outward to `/wholesale` or `/shop` with transactional anchor text.
